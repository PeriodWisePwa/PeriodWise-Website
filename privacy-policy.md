# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

At PeriodWise, we respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information may be handled when you use our website, tools, and resources.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information You Provide

You may browse most of the PeriodWise website without creating an account or providing personal information.

If you voluntarily provide information through a PeriodWise form, such as when signing up to receive a free guide or other resources, we may receive information such as your email address.

We use information you voluntarily provide only for the purposes explained at the time of collection, such as delivering requested resources or communicating with you when you have chosen to receive communications.

## 2. Email Communications

PeriodWise may use a third-party email service provider to manage email subscriptions and deliver requested resources.

If you subscribe to a PeriodWise email list, your email address may be processed by our email service provider on our behalf.

You can unsubscribe from marketing or newsletter communications by using the unsubscribe option included in applicable emails.

## 3. PeriodWise App and Cycle Information

The PeriodWise App is designed as a private cycle-tracking and educational tool.

Information entered into the app may include cycle dates and other information you choose to enter.

PeriodWise does not require you to provide your name or email address to use the core app.

Where the app stores information locally on your device, that information remains subject to the storage, security, and privacy settings of your device and browser.

You should avoid entering information into any tool that you do not want stored on your device.

## 4. Website Analytics and Cookies

PeriodWise may use analytics and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the website, improve our content, and maintain website functionality.

Some technologies may collect information such as:

- Browser type
- Device type
- General location information
- Pages visited
- Referring website
- Approximate time spent on pages
- Other technical information about website usage

Third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Links

PeriodWise may link to or use third-party services, including email providers, payment platforms, cloud-storage services, affiliate websites, social-media platforms, and other external websites.

When you leave the PeriodWise website or interact directly with a third-party service, that service may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

PeriodWise does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

## 6. Free Guides and Digital Resources

PeriodWise may provide free guides, educational materials, calculators, and other digital resources.

Some resources may be delivered through third-party services.

For example, a free guide may be hosted or delivered through an external document or file-hosting service. Accessing such a resource may be subject to that provider's privacy policy and terms.

## 7. Affiliate Links

Some PeriodWise content may contain affiliate links.

If you purchase a product through an affiliate link, PeriodWise may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate partners may use cookies or other technologies to attribute purchases or referrals according to their own policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PeriodWise is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable privacy laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to PeriodWise, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through PeriodWise services.

However, no website, online service, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate security practices when using online services and protect access to your own devices and accounts.

## 10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the service you use, you may have choices regarding your information.

These may include:

- Choosing not to provide optional personal information
- Unsubscribing from email communications
- Managing cookies through your browser settings
- Choosing not to use third-party services
- Requesting information about personal information that PeriodWise may hold about you, where applicable

Some privacy rights may vary depending on your location and applicable law.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by PeriodWise, please contact us through our [Contact page]({{ "/contact/" | relative_url }}).
