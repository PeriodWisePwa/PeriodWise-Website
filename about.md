# About PeriodWise

## Understand Your Cycle. Understand Your Body.

PeriodWise is a women's health education and digital wellness brand created to help women better understand their menstrual cycles, fertility awareness, and everyday cycle-related health information.

We believe that understanding your cycle can make it easier to recognize your own patterns, ask better questions, and make more informed decisions about your health.

## What PeriodWise Offers

PeriodWise brings together educational resources and practical tools designed around menstrual and reproductive health awareness.

Our resources include:

- Menstrual cycle education
- Fertility awareness information
- Cycle tracking tools
- Fertile window education and calculators
- Practical guides and digital resources
- Cycle tracking journals and workbooks
- Educational videos and other learning resources

Our goal is to make useful information easier to understand and practical tools easier to access.

## Our Approach

Women's menstrual cycles are not identical.

Cycle length, symptoms, bleeding patterns, and other experiences can vary from person to person and can also change over time.

For this reason, PeriodWise focuses on **education, awareness, and tracking** rather than promising a particular health outcome.

We encourage you to learn about your own patterns, keep useful records, and seek professional medical advice when something concerns you.

## Privacy Matters

PeriodWise is built with privacy in mind.

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information and to make our tools and resources as straightforward as possible.

When you use a PeriodWise service that involves another provider, such as an email signup, payment platform, or external resource, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy.

For more information about how information is handled on this website, please read our [Privacy Policy]({{ "/privacy-policy/" | relative_url }}).

## Educational Information, Not Medical Advice

PeriodWise provides general educational and informational content.

Our articles, guides, calculators, videos, and other resources are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

A calculator or tracking tool can help you organize information and understand general cycle concepts, but it should not be treated as a medical diagnosis or a guarantee of fertility or pregnancy outcomes.

If you have concerns about your menstrual cycle, fertility, pregnancy, pain, bleeding, or other symptoms, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

## The PeriodWise Team

PeriodWise is developed as a brand focused on making menstrual and reproductive health education more accessible, practical, and understandable.

Our aim is simple:

**Help you understand your cycle.  
Help you understand your body.**
