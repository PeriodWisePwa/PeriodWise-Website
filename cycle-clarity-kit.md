## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this a physical product?

No. The Cycle Clarity Kit is a digital product.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The final digital format will be designed to be accessible from a phone, tablet or computer.

### Is this medical advice?

No. PeriodWise resources are educational tools and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

### Can this guarantee when I will ovulate?

No. Menstrual cycles can vary, and no educational resource can guarantee the exact timing of ovulation for every individual.
